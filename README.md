## Key Findings
- Average MRR growth in 2024: **7.61%**  
- Industry benchmark: **15%**  
- Company is underperforming, with growth lagging behind target in Q1–Q3.  
- Q4 showed some recovery (12.22%), but still below industry average.  

## Business Implications
- Slowing growth puts pressure on revenue targets and investor confidence.  
- Without corrective measures, the company risks losing market competitiveness.  

## Recommendations
- **Expand into new market segments** (primary solution).  
- Diversify product offerings to attract broader customer base.  
- Improve customer retention by investing in support and engagement programs.  
- Leverage pricing optimization to maximize recurring revenue.
